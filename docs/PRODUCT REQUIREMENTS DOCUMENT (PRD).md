# **PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS DOCUMENT (PRD)**

## **DAYLY — Your AI-Powered Everyday Life Companion**

**Product Name:** DAYLY  
**Tagline:** **Make Today Count.**  
**Product Category:** Everyday Productivity / Personal Organization  
**Primary Users:** Busy professionals and entrepreneurs/business owners  
**Product Type:** AI-powered personal productivity companion  
**Document Version:** 1.0

---

# **1\. Product Overview**

**DAYLY** is an AI-powered everyday life companion designed to help busy professionals and entrepreneurs organize their responsibilities, determine what matters most, create a realistic daily plan, and stay productive throughout the day.

Instead of requiring users to manually organize multiple tasks, deadlines, schedules, goals, and personal responsibilities, DAYLY allows users to simply **type, speak, or manually enter what they need to accomplish**.

DAYLY then turns that information into an intelligent daily plan.

### **Core promise**

> **Tell DAYLY what you need to do. DAYLY helps you decide what matters, plan your day, and get it done.**

---

# **2\. Problem Statement**

Busy professionals and entrepreneurs often have many responsibilities competing for their attention.

They may need to:

* Attend meetings  
* Complete work assignments  
* Respond to clients  
* Manage a business  
* Make phone calls  
* Study or learn new skills  
* Exercise  
* Handle household responsibilities  
* Remember appointments  
* Work toward personal goals

These responsibilities are often scattered across calendars, notebooks, messaging apps, reminder apps, task managers, and memory.

The result can be:

* Overwhelm  
* Procrastination  
* Missed deadlines  
* Forgotten tasks  
* Poor prioritization  
* Inefficient use of time  
* Difficulty maintaining habits and long-term goals

**DAYLY solves this by bringing everyday responsibilities into one simple experience and helping users determine what deserves their attention.**

---

# **3\. Product Vision**

DAYLY's vision is to become the **personal command center for everyday life**.

The product should eventually help users answer four important questions every day:

> **What do I need to do?**  
> **What matters most?**  
> **When should I do it?**  
> **How am I progressing?**

---

# **4\. Product Mission**

To help busy people **organize their responsibilities, focus on what matters, and make consistent progress every day.**

# **7\. Core Product Experience**

The fundamental DAYLY experience should follow this journey:

### **1\. Capture**

The user tells DAYLY what they need to do.

They can:

* Type  
* Speak  
* Add manually

Example:

> “I need to finish my proposal, call three clients, attend my meeting at 2 PM, exercise, and prepare tomorrow's presentation.”

### **2\. Understand**

DAYLY interprets the user's responsibilities.

### **3\. Prioritize**

DAYLY determines which activities are:

* Urgent  
* Important  
* Time-sensitive  
* Flexible  
* Optional

### **4\. Plan**

DAYLY creates a practical schedule around the user's available time and fixed commitments.

### **5\. Execute**

DAYLY helps the user stay focused and reminds them about upcoming responsibilities.

### **6\. Adapt**

If something changes, DAYLY adjusts the plan.

Example:

> “My 2 PM meeting has been moved to 4 PM.”

DAYLY reorganizes the remaining day.

### **7\. Review**

At the end of the day, DAYLY helps the user review what happened and prepare for tomorrow.

---

# **8\. Core Features**

## **Feature 1: AI Daily Planner ⭐**

This is the **heart of DAYLY**.

DAYLY creates a personalized daily plan based on:

* Tasks  
* Deadlines  
* Fixed appointments  
* Importance  
* Urgency  
* Available time  
* User preferences  
* Goals  
* Existing commitments

### **Example**

The user says:

> “I have a proposal to finish, three clients to call, a 2 PM meeting, groceries to buy and I want to exercise.”

DAYLY could respond:

> **Good morning\! Here's your plan for today.**

> 🔴 **9:00 AM — Finish proposal**  
> 🟠 **11:00 AM — Call clients**  
> 🍽️ **12:30 PM — Lunch & break**  
> 🔴 **2:00 PM — Client meeting**  
> 🟡 **4:00 PM — Buy groceries**  
> 🟢 **6:00 PM — Exercise**

> **Today's main focus:** Finish the proposal and prepare for your meeting.

# **9\. Smart Task Management**

Users can create and manage tasks.

Each task can have:

* Task name  
* Deadline  
* Priority  
* Estimated duration  
* Status  
* Category  
* Notes

Users should be able to:

* Add tasks  
* Complete tasks  
* Edit tasks  
* Delete tasks  
* Move tasks  
* Postpone tasks

The important difference is that **DAYLY should help manage the task rather than simply store it.**

---

# **10\. AI Prioritization**

DAYLY should automatically help users determine which tasks deserve attention first.

For example:

### **High Priority**

Finish proposal due today.

### **Medium Priority**

Reply to a non-urgent client message.

### **Low Priority**

Organize old files.

The user should also be able to override DAYLY's suggestions.

### **Principle**

> **AI recommends. The user decides.**

---

# **11\. Smart Scheduling**

After prioritizing tasks, DAYLY creates a realistic schedule.

It should consider:

* Fixed appointments  
* Available working hours  
* Task duration  
* Breaks  
* Deadlines  
* Personal preferences

DAYLY should avoid creating unrealistic schedules containing more work than the user can reasonably complete.

---

# **12\. Flexible Rescheduling**

Life changes.

A user may say:

> “I couldn't finish my proposal.”

DAYLY should help reorganize the remaining responsibilities.

For example:

> “No problem. I've moved the proposal to tomorrow morning and adjusted your other tasks.”

This is an important part of DAYLY's identity.

**DAYLY should adapt to the user's life rather than forcing the user to adapt to a rigid schedule.**

---

# **13\. Smart Reminders**

DAYLY should remind users about important responsibilities without becoming annoying.

Reminder types could include:

* Upcoming task  
* Appointment  
* Deadline  
* Unfinished important task  
* Habit  
* Goal  
* Daily check-in

The emphasis should be on **useful reminders rather than constant notifications.**

---

# **14\. Morning Check-In**

Each day can begin with a short personalized briefing.

Example:

> **Good morning\! ☀️**

> You have 7 things planned today.

> **Today's top priorities:**

> 1. Finish your business proposal  
> 2. Attend your 2 PM meeting  
> 3. Call your three clients

> You have 8 hours available today.

> **Let's make today count.**

This creates a strong daily habit around DAYLY.

---

# **15\. Focus Mode**

When the user starts an important task, DAYLY can shift attention toward that task.

Example:

> **Focus Time**

> 🎯 Finish Business Proposal  
> Estimated time: 60 minutes

> **You've got this. Let's get it done.**

The purpose is to help users avoid constantly jumping between responsibilities.

---

# **16\. Progress Tracking**

DAYLY should show users how they are progressing.

Possible indicators:

* Tasks completed  
* Tasks remaining  
* Goals progressing  
* Habits completed  
* Daily productivity streak  
* Weekly progress

The dashboard should be encouraging rather than judgmental.

---

# **17\. Goals**

Users can create meaningful longer-term goals.

Examples:

> **Business:** Get 20 new clients this quarter.

> **Career:** Complete a professional course.

> **Personal:** Exercise four times every week.

DAYLY can then help connect daily actions with these larger goals.

This is important because it gives everyday tasks **purpose**.

---

# **18\. Habit Tracking**

Users can create recurring habits.

Examples:

* Exercise  
* Read  
* Pray/meditate  
* Study  
* Drink water  
* Journal  
* Learn a skill  
* Review finances

DAYLY can incorporate these into the daily plan.

---

# **19\. Evening Review**

At the end of the day, DAYLY can ask:

> **How did today go?**

It can show:

**Completed:** 6 tasks  
**Moved:** 2 tasks  
**Missed:** 1 task

Then ask:

> “Would you like me to organize the remaining tasks for tomorrow?”

This naturally leads into the next day's planning.

---

# **20\. Conversational AI Assistant**

The user should be able to interact with DAYLY naturally.

Examples:

> “What should I focus on now?”

> “Move my meeting to Friday.”

> “What haven't I completed today?”

> “What are my priorities tomorrow?”

> “I have only two hours left today. What should I do?”

> “Add exercise to my daily routine.”

> “I don't have enough time to finish everything. Help me decide what to move.”

This conversational experience is one of DAYLY's biggest opportunities for differentiation.

The MVP should prove one central idea:

> **Can DAYLY take a user's responsibilities and turn them into a useful, personalized daily plan that helps the user actually get things done?**

### **MVP Features**

**1\. User task capture**

* Type  
* Voice  
* Manual entry

**2\. AI task understanding**

**3\. AI prioritization**

**4\. AI daily planning**

**5\. Task management**

**6\. Smart reminders**

**7\. Rescheduling**

**8\. Basic goals**

**9\. Basic habit tracking**

**10\. Morning check-in**

**11\. Evening review**

That is enough to establish the core DAYLY experience.

### **Morning**

**Check in → Review priorities → Start first task**

↓

### **During the day**

**Complete → Receive useful reminders → Adjust schedule when necessary**

↓

### **Afternoon**

**Check progress → Reprioritize if necessary**

↓

### **Evening**

**Review → Complete/move remaining tasks → Prepare tomorrow**

↓

### **Next morning**

**New personalized plan**

This creates DAYLY's core **daily engagement loop**.

# **26\. Key User Experience Principle**

DAYLY should never make users feel like they have **another app to manage**.

Instead:

> **DAYLY should manage the organization for them.**

The product should feel:

* Simple  
* Personal  
* Helpful  
* Intelligent  
* Encouraging  
* Flexible  
* Non-judgmental

---

# **27\. What Makes DAYLY Different**

There are many task managers and calendar applications.

DAYLY's differentiation should be the combination of:

### **Capture → Understand → Prioritize → Plan → Remind → Adapt → Review**

Most traditional productivity tools require users to do much of the organizing themselves.

DAYLY's promise is:

> **“Don't just give me a list. Help me figure out what to do.”**

That should become the heart of the brand.

---

# **28\. Success Metrics**

The first version should focus on whether DAYLY actually helps users manage their days.

Important measures include:

### **Daily engagement**

How many users return to DAYLY each day?

### **Plan creation**

How often do users create or accept a daily plan?

### **Task completion**

How many planned tasks are completed?

### **Rescheduling**

How often do users successfully adjust plans rather than abandoning them?

### **Retention**

Do users continue using DAYLY after their first week?

### **User satisfaction**

Do users feel DAYLY makes their day easier?

The most important question is:

> **Does DAYLY become something users naturally return to every day?**

---

# **29\. Product Personality**

DAYLY should behave like a **smart, supportive personal assistant**.

Not:

> “You failed to complete 3 tasks.”

Instead:

> “You still have three tasks remaining. Since your day is almost over, I recommend moving two of them to tomorrow.”

Not:

> “You missed your habit.”

Instead:

> “You missed today's workout. Would you like to move it to tomorrow?”

The AI should help users recover rather than make them feel guilty.

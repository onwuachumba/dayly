# DAYLY — Make Today Count.

**Your AI-powered everyday life companion.**

> Tell DAYLY what you need to do. DAYLY helps you decide what matters, plan your day, and get it done.

---

## 1. Product Overview

**DAYLY** is an AI-powered everyday life companion designed to help busy professionals and entrepreneurs organize their responsibilities, determine what matters most, create a realistic daily plan, and stay productive throughout the day.

Instead of requiring users to manually organize tasks, deadlines, schedules, goals, and personal responsibilities across calendars, notebooks, messaging apps, and memory — DAYLY lets users simply **type, speak, or manually enter what they need to accomplish**, then turns that into an intelligent daily plan.

**Product Type:** Everyday Productivity / Personal Organization
**Document Version:** Initial version (see `docs/PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS DOCUMENT (PRD).md` v1.0)

### Core promise

> **DAYLY turns everything you need to do into a personalized plan that tells you what matters, when to do it, and helps you stay on track.**

---

## 2. The Problem

Busy professionals and entrepreneurs juggle competing responsibilities:

- Meetings, work assignments, client follow-ups
- Managing a business, phone calls, studying / upskilling
- Exercise, household responsibilities, appointments
- Personal goals and long-term habits

These are scattered across calendars, notebooks, reminder apps, task managers, and memory. The result:

- Overwhelm and procrastination
- Missed deadlines and forgotten tasks
- Poor prioritization
- Inefficient use of time
- Difficulty maintaining habits and long-term goals

These users don't need **more productivity tools**. They need something that helps them **decide what deserves attention and turn intentions into action.**

---

## 3. The Solution

DAYLY brings everyday responsibilities into one simple experience:

**Capture → Understand → Prioritize → Plan → Remind → Adapt → Review**

1. **Capture** — User types, speaks, or manually adds responsibilities.
   e.g. *"I need to finish my proposal, call three clients, attend my meeting at 2 PM, exercise, and prepare tomorrow's presentation."*
2. **Understand** — DAYLY interprets tasks, deadlines, fixed appointments, and context.
3. **Prioritize** — DAYLY determines what is urgent, important, time-sensitive, flexible, or optional. Principle: **AI recommends. The user decides.**
4. **Plan** — DAYLY creates a practical schedule around available time and fixed commitments. No unrealistic overload.
5. **Remind** — Useful, non-annoying reminders for upcoming tasks, deadlines, habits, and daily check-ins. Includes Morning Check-In and Focus Mode.
6. **Adapt** — When life changes (e.g. *"My 2 PM meeting moved to 4 PM"* or *"I couldn't finish my proposal"*), DAYLY reorganizes the remaining day.
7. **Review** — Evening Review shows completed / moved / missed, and prepares tomorrow. Progress tracking stays encouraging, never judgmental.

Differentiation:

> **"Don't just give me a list. Help me figure out what to do."**

Most task managers store lists. DAYLY manages the organization for the user.

## 5. Core Features

Based on PRD v1.0:

- **AI Daily Planner ⭐ (heart of DAYLY)**
  Personalized daily plan from tasks, deadlines, fixed appointments, importance, urgency, available time, preferences, goals, and commitments.
  Example output: 9:00 AM Finish proposal, 11:00 AM Call clients, 12:30 PM Lunch & break, 2:00 PM Client meeting, 4:00 PM Groceries, 6:00 PM Exercise + *"Today's main focus..."*

- **Smart Task Management**
  Add / complete / edit / delete / move / postpone. Each task: name, deadline, priority, estimated duration, status, category, notes. DAYLY helps manage the task, not just store it.

- **AI Prioritization**
  Auto-suggests High / Medium / Low with user override. e.g. proposal due today = High, non-urgent client message = Medium, organize old files = Low.

- **Smart Scheduling**
  Realistic schedule considering fixed appointments, working hours, durations, breaks, deadlines, preferences.

- **Flexible Rescheduling**
  e.g. *"No problem. I've moved the proposal to tomorrow morning and adjusted your other tasks."* DAYLY adapts to life, not vice versa.

- **Smart Reminders**
  Upcoming tasks, appointments, deadlines, unfinished important tasks, habits, goals, daily check-in — useful, not constant.

- **Morning Check-In**
  Personalized briefing: *"Good morning! ☀️ You have 7 things planned today. Today's top priorities: 1... 2... 3... Let's make today count."*

- **Focus Mode**
  Dedicated view when starting an important task: goal, estimated time, encouragement. Reduces context-switching.

- **Progress Tracking**
  Tasks completed / remaining, goal progress, habits completed, streaks, weekly progress. Encouraging dashboard.

- **Goals**
  Longer-term goals (e.g. *Get 20 new clients this quarter*, *Complete a professional course*, *Exercise 4x/week*). Daily actions linked to larger purpose.

- **Habit Tracking**
  Recurring habits (exercise, read, pray/meditate, study, journal, etc.) incorporated into daily plan.

- **Evening Review**
  *"How did today go?"* Completed / Moved / Missed summary + *"Would you like me to organize the remaining tasks for tomorrow?"*

- **Conversational AI Assistant**
  Natural queries: *"What should I focus on now?"*, *"Move my meeting to Friday."*, *"What haven't I completed today?"*, *"I have only two hours left today. What should I do?"*

- **Main Dashboard**
  Greeting, Today's Progress (e.g. 4 of 7), Top Priority, Today's Schedule, Goals, Habits, Ask DAYLY prompt.

## 6. MVP — Minimum Viable Product

The MVP proves one central idea:

> **Can DAYLY take a user's responsibilities and turn them into a useful, personalized daily plan that helps the user actually get things done?**

MVP scope (12 items):

1. User task capture (type / voice / manual)
2. AI task understanding
3. AI prioritization
4. AI daily planning
5. Task management
6. Smart reminders
7. Rescheduling
8. Basic goals
9. Basic habit tracking
10. Morning check-in
11. Evening review
12. Simple progress dashboard

Out of scope for MVP (Phase 2+): deeper calendar integration, weekly planning, productivity insights / patterns, advanced goal planning, sophisticated habit coaching, multiple life areas, finance / wellness / family / travel expansion.

---

## 7. Product Vision

To become the **personal command center for everyday life** — helping users answer every day:

> **What do I need to do?**
> **What matters most?**
> **When should I do it?**
> **How am I progressing?**

**Mission:** Help busy people organize their responsibilities, focus on what matters, and make consistent progress every day.

**North Star experience:** *A busy entrepreneur opens DAYLY in the morning, tells it everything that needs to happen today, and within seconds gets a realistic plan showing what matters most and what to do next.*

**Personality:** Smart, supportive personal assistant. Helps users recover, never guilts. e.g. not *"You failed..."* but *"You still have three tasks remaining. Since your day is almost over, I recommend moving two to tomorrow."*

**Success metrics:** Daily engagement, plan creation/acceptance rate, task completion, successful reschedules vs. abandons, week-1 retention, user satisfaction — ultimately: *Does DAYLY become something users naturally return to every day?*
